Privacy Policy

High Limit Blackjack · Effective November 19, 2026

High Limit Blackjack itself does not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There are no accounts or sign-in.

Your game data stays on your iPhone

Your bankroll, statistics, training results and settings are saved only on your iPhone, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Ads

Until you buy Remove Ads, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. They are non-personalized: they are not based on your interests or activity in other apps, and the app does not ask to track you.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as your IP address (used to estimate your general location), device identifiers, the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent first, and can change your choice at any time in Game Settings > Privacy Choices.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. Once it's bought, the app stops loading ads altogether.

Play money only

High Limit Blackjack offers no real-money gambling or prizes, and chips can't be bought.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
